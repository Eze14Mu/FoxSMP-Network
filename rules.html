Game rules

Read this before you join

Broken rules get reported to Roblox as well as enforced in-game where required — this keeps the game itself in good standing.

General conduct

  • 1.1 Follow the Roblox Community Standards at all times — our rules don't override them.
  • 1.2 No harassment, hate speech, or targeted bullying in chat or voice.
  • 1.3 No spamming chat or exploiting chat filters.
  • 1.4 Keep personal information out of public chat.

Fair play

  • 2.1 Exploiting, script injection, or third-party clients results in a permanent ban.
  • 2.2 Team-killing or intentionally sabotaging teammates outside game mechanics is bannable.
  • 2.3 Boosting stats through alt accounts voids leaderboard placement.

Trading & purchases

  • 3.1 All Robux purchases are final — no in-game refunds outside Roblox's own policy.
  • 3.2 Real-money trading for in-game items is not permitted.
