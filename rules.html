Server rules

Read this before you spawn in

Punishments are logged and reviewed by staff. A genuine mistake handled honestly usually gets leniency.

General conduct

  • 1.1 No hate speech, harassment, or targeted discrimination.
  • 1.2 No spamming chat, commands, or the server with bots or macros.
  • 1.3 Impersonating staff or other players is an instant ban.
  • 1.4 Keep real-world personal information out of public chat.

Building & land

  • 2.1 Claim your build before leaving it unattended for more than 7 days.
  • 2.2 Griefing unclaimed builds is allowed unless staff has flagged the area as protected.
  • 2.3 No lag-machines — ask staff before large-scale redstone or farms.

PvP & combat

  • 3.1 Combat logging results in a temp ban.
  • 3.2 Killing inside a marked safezone is bannable.
  • 3.3 Any client modification giving an unfair advantage is an instant permanent ban.

Trading & economy

  • 4.1 Real-money trading for in-game items or currency is not permitted.
  • 4.2 Duplication glitches must be reported, not used.
